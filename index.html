How To Use IntellectFlow

Beginner guide
IntellectFlow helps you study a little every day without overloading your schedule. Add topics, review what is due, and let the app decide when each topic should come back.
1. Set your daily capacity

At the top-right, set how many minutes you can realistically study today. Start small (for example: 30 to 60 minutes).

2. Add topics

Open the Topics tab, add a topic name, optional category, and session length. Add everything you want to learn over time.

3. Follow today's agenda

In Dashboard, complete due topics one by one. If total load is too high, the app defers extra items to reduce burnout.

4. Choose honest review feedback

After each review, either click the result that matches your memory: Struggled, Okay, or Mastered. Or use Assess with AI to answer generated questions from your material.

5. Let spaced repetition do the rest

Topics you struggle with come back sooner. Strong topics get pushed further out. New or forgotten topics go through short learning steps before longer intervals.

6. Use AI checks when you want objective feedback

On a due topic, click Assess with AI, paste your material, answer the generated questions, and apply the result. Longer material gives more questions automatically. Existing quizzes are reused unless you click Regenerate Quiz.

7. Check progress in Analytics

Use Analytics to track study consistency, difficulty mix, and SRS quality metrics like retention proxy and overdue rate.

8. Keep your data safe

Use Backup (.json) regularly. If you study on multiple devices, sign in on the Sync tab.
